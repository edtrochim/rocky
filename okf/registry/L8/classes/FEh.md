# FEh Evergreen hill forest

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Natural forest area within the reserve forest boundary and protected forest boundary of Chittagong Hill Tracts (CHT) is known as hill forest. It consists of moist tropical evergreen and semi-evergreen trees. Tree cover ranges from 80% - 100% and top canopy of trees reach a height of 25-62 m.
