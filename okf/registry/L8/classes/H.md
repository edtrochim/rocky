# H Herb dominated area

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

The geographic area which is dominated by grass with very little to no woody vegetation is called herb dominated area. These types of vegetation are generally found in newly accreted land, year round fallow land or adjacent to the international boundary of Bangladesh. The coverage is 20 - 100%.
