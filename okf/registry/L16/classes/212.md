# 212 Permanently irrigated land

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Crops irrigated permanently or periodically, using a permanent infrastructure (irrigation channels, drainage network).Most of these crops cannot be cultivated without an artificial water supply. Does not include sporadically irrigated land.
