# 211 Non irrigated arable land

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Cereals, legumes, fodder crops, root crops and fallow land. Includes flowers and fruit trees (nurseries cultivation) and vegetables, whether open field, under plastic or glass (includes market gardening). Includes aromatic, medicinal and culinary plants. Does not include permanent pastures.
