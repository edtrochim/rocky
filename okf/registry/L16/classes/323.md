# 323 Sclerophillous vegetation

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Bushy sclerophyllous vegetation, includes maquis and garrigue. In case of shrub vegetation areas composed of sclerophyllous species such as Juniperus oxycedrus and heathland species such as Buxus spp. or Ostrya carpinifolia with no visible dominance (each species occupy about 50% of the area), priority will be given to sclerophyllous vegetation and the whole area will be assigned class 323.
