# H Herb dominated area

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Herbs dominated areas are lands dominated by grass rather than shrubs or trees. These grasses are typically found in areas with shallow, rocky soil or steep slopes. These marginal lands may serve as pastures and are often overexploited by sheep and goats, leading to nutrient deficiency and severe erosion. These areas are managed by local communities or treated as government-owned land. This category is divided into: (1) Moderately dense herbaceous vegetation and (2) Sparse herbaceous vegetation
