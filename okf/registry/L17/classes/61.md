# 61 Forested wetland

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Forested Wetlands are wetlands dominated by woody vegetation. Forested Wetland includes season-ally flooded bottomland hardwoods, mangrove swamps, shrub swamps, and wooded swamps ineluding those around bogs.
