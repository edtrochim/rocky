# 2 - 1 Cropland and pasture

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

The several components of Cropland and Pasture now used for agricultural statistics include: crop land harvested, including bush fruits; cultivated summer-fallow and idle cropland; land on which crop failure occurs; cropland in soil-improvement grasses and legumes; cropland used only for pasture in rotation with crops; and past~re on land more or less permanently used for that purpose.
