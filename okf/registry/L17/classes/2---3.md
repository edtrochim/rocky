# 2 - 3 Confined feeding operations

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Confined Feeding Operations are large, specialized livestock production enterprises, chiefly beef cattle feedlots, dairy operations with confined feeding, and large poultry farms, but also including hog feedlots. These operations have large animal populations restricted to relatively small areas.
