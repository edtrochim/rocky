# 32 Shrub and brush rangeland

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

The typical shrub occurrences are found in those arid and semiarid regions characterized by such xerophytic vegetative types with woody stems as big sagebrush (Artemisia tridentata), shadscale , greasewood (Sarcobatus vermiculatus), or creosote bush (Larrea divaricata) andalso by the typical desert succulent xerophytes, such as the various forms of Cactus.
