# 1 Artificial Surfaces

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

The class is composed of any type of areas with a predominant artificial surface.Any urban or related feature is included in this class, for example urban parks (parks, parkland, sport facilities). The class also includes industrial areas, waste dump deposit and extraction sites.
