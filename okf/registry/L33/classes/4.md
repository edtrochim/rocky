# 4 Tree Covered Area

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

This class includes any geographic area dominated by natural tree plants with a cover of 10% or more. Other types of plants (shrubs and/or herbs) can be present, even with a density higher than trees. Areas planted with trees for afforestation purposes and forest plantations are included in this class. This class includes areas seasonally or permanently flooded with fresh water. It excludes coastal mangroves (→07).
