# 5 Shrubs Covered Area

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

This class includes any geographical area dominated by natural shrubs having a cover of 10% or more. Trees can be present in scattered form if their cover is less than 10%. Herbaceous plants can also be present at any density. The class includes shrub covered areas permanently or regularly flooded by inland fresh water. It excludes shrubs flooded by salt or brackish water in coastal areas (→07).
