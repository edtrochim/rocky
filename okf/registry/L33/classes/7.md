# 7 Mangroves

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

This class includes any geographical area dominated by woody vegetation (trees and/or shrubs) with a cover of 10% or more that is permanently or regularly flooded by salt and/or brackish water located in the coastal areas or in the deltas of rivers.
