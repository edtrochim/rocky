# Ri Rice field

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

This is rice production. This cereal production can be classified into both rainfed and irrigated crops. Depending on the type of variety, rice can be grown in the rainy season using rainwater, or in the dry season. In the second case, production takes place in the shallows. It is traditionally practiced in lowlands without development but is also practiced in lowlands equipped with hydro-agricultural development allowing the supply of water for production outside the rainy season. The latter case is easily identifiable in the images. On the other hand, rainfed rice blends in with other crops, making its identification more difficult.
