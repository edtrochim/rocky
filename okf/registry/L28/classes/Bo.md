# Bo Bushland open

## Definition (verbatim, FAO LCLR)

Land not defined as "forest", shrubs height between 1-3 m sometimes combined with trees. It does not include land that is predominantly under agricultural or urban land use. Used for hunting, recreation, and grazing.
