## Framing summary

MapBiomas Argentina (Collection 2) is produced by the MapBiomas Argentina initiative for annual land cover and land use monitoring. It separates 3 Closed forests from 4 Open forests and splits shrublands into 66 Closed shrublands, 77 Open shrublands and 63 Shrubs and herbaceous mosaic, a canopy-based vocabulary consistent with how Argentina's native forest law defines forest by cover rather than by biome. Evidence is as far as the legend code sheet supports it; the ATBD should be checked before accepting these nodes.
