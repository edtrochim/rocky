# Argentine Native Forest Law (Ley 26.331, 2007)

Government of Argentina · law · Argentina 2007

## Effect on classes

The law's minimum-budget regime for native forests is applied through canopy-cover-based forest definitions and provincial land-use zoning, which is consistent with the closed/open forest split (3/4) and the treatment of open shrublands as non-forest; the thresholds themselves are not stated in the legend sheet. Likely instrument; not cited in the source.
