# Brazilian Forest Code (Lei 12.651/2012) and native-vegetation policy

Government of Brazil · law · Brazil 2012

## Effect on classes

The distinction between natural formations (Forest Formation, Savanna Formation, Wetland, Grassland) and anthropic uses (Pasture, Agriculture, Forest Plantation, Mosaic of Uses) is what the map is used for under native-vegetation protection rules; the aggregate 'Farming' (14) versus 'Forest' (1) is the deforestation-monitoring boundary. Likely instrument; not cited in the legend sheet.
