# MapBiomas annual mapping programme, Brazil (Collection 10)

MapBiomas network · programme · Brazil 2025

## Effect on classes

Defines the six level-1 aggregates (1 Forest, 10 Herbaceous and Shrubby Vegetation, 14 Farming, 22 Non vegetated area, 26 Water, 27 Not Observed) and the crop-level splits (Soybean, Sugar cane, Rice, Cotton, Coffee, Citrus, Palm Oil) that serve agricultural monitoring; 'beta' classes (Cotton, Photovoltaic Power Plant) are experimental.
