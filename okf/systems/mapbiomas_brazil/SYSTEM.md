## Framing summary

MapBiomas Brazil is produced by the MapBiomas network (a consortium of universities, NGOs and technology companies) for annual land use and land cover monitoring of Brazil. Its classes follow Brazilian biome and vegetation vocabulary (Forest Formation, Savanna Formation, Wetland, Mangrove, Restinga) and separate agricultural uses by crop because the collections serve deforestation monitoring, agricultural statistics and land-use policy. Class names such as Savanna Formation carry legally and phytogeographically defined Brazilian terms (the Cerrado biome), so they are not directly comparable with 'open forest' classes in neighbouring systems. Evidence for each instrument below is stated as far as the source given here (the legend code sheet) supports it; the ATBD should be checked before these framing nodes are accepted.
