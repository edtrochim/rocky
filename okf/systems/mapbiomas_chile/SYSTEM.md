## Framing summary

MapBiomas Chile (Collection 2) is produced by the MapBiomas Chile initiative for annual land cover and land use monitoring. Its forest classes split 59 Primary Forest, 60 Secondary Forest and 67 Dwarf forest, a distinction that follows Chile's native forest policy vocabulary (bosque nativo adulto, renoval, bosque achaparrado) rather than a physical cover threshold, and it carries Patagonian and Andean classes (Steppe, Salt Flat, Ice and snow) that neighbouring systems lack. Evidence is as far as the legend code sheet supports it; the collection's ATBD should be checked before accepting these nodes.
