# Chilean Native Forest Law (Ley 20.283, 2008) and forest-type vocabulary

Government of Chile (CONAF) · law · Chile 2008

## Effect on classes

The split of 59 Primary Forest / 60 Secondary Forest / 67 Dwarf forest follows the native-forest categories used in Chilean forest policy (bosque nativo adulto, renoval, bosque achaparrado), which govern management plans and subsidies. Physically, primary and secondary forest are both LC_Tree stands; the split is a framing-only split. Likely instrument; the legend sheet does not cite the law.
